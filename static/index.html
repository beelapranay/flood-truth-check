Why the obvious checks do not work

What we tried first

Check a claim against its exact property. Public NFIP claims data rounds every location to about a seven mile box to protect privacy, so there is no exact address to check.

Why the next idea broke

Flag claims whose flood zone was never updated after a remap. NFIP has an official grandfathering rule that lets policyholders keep their old zone on purpose, so a mismatch there is usually legal, not an error.

What works instead

Compare a claim's stated risk against Mireye's independently measured terrain today. A claim calling itself coastal high hazard on terrain that sits high, dry, and miles from water is a contradiction regardless of any rule about zones.

How it works

01

Pull claims

Recent NFIP claims come straight from FEMA's public OpenFEMA claims API — no scraping, no private data.

02

Check the ground

Each claim's location is sent to Mireye for cited elevation, floodplain, coastal, and wetland facts, sourced straight from USGS, FEMA, and NOAA.

03

Rank contradictions

Claims need two or more independent signals to disagree before they are flagged, then get ranked by contradiction strength and payout size.
